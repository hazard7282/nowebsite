Minimal, Deep Tech & Rominimal DJ in Florida

Adam Bogdan — Romanian-born, Miami-based DJ, producer and founder of Rapture's Deli. Minimal tech, deep tech, rominimal and groove-driven underground house for clubs, festivals and curated events across Florida.

Miami-based · Florida booking inquiries welcome

Booking lane: underground clubs, festivals, label showcases, after-hours sessions and curated events looking for minimal, deep tech, rominimal, deep house or stripped-back groove rather than commercial EDM.

I am Adam Bogdan, a Romanian-born DJ and producer based in Miami with more than two decades behind the decks. My sets combine the patience and detail of Romanian minimal with minimal-house swing, deep-tech pressure and a broader underground house vocabulary.

I also run Rapture's Deli, a Miami label and event platform connecting local selectors with international underground artists. Recent Florida activity includes Miami lineups with Barac, Lizz, Suciu and Sublee.

What I play

Minimal Tech

Tight percussion, rolling bass and restrained arrangements built for long blends.

Deep Tech

More low-end weight and club pressure without losing space or groove.

Rominimal

Romanian-rooted minimal: hypnotic, detailed, patient and built around micro-rhythm.

Underground House

Deep house, minimal house and groove-led records used to widen the set without breaking its identity.

Florida booking fit

The strongest fit is a room where programming matters: underground clubs, festivals, art/music concepts, label nights, after-hours sessions and events that want a selector rather than a playlist of obvious hits.

Miami is my home base. For Orlando, Tampa Bay, St. Petersburg, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach or other Florida markets, send the date, city, venue, set length and event concept through the booking contact.
